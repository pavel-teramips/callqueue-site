# CallQueue — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-07-27*

CallQueue is a personal call-back queue. Privacy summary: **your data never
leaves your phone.**

## What the app accesses and why

- **Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)** — used only to (a) let you pick a person to add
  to your queue, (b) show their name and photo in the queue, and (c) find the
  WhatsApp calling entry for that contact so a tap can start the call. Contact
  data is read on demand and stored only in the app's private storage on your
  device (the queue entries you created).
- **Nearby devices / Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT)** — used only to let you
  choose which paired Bluetooth device is your car, and to notice when the
  phone connects to it so the app can remind you who is waiting.
- **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)** — used only for the driving reminder.

## What the app collects, shares, or transmits

**Nothing.** CallQueue has no server, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no
tracking, and makes no network connections. All data (your queue, your chosen
car device) is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall
the app.

## Third parties

None. Tapping a queue entry hands the call over to WhatsApp (or your phone's
dialer), which then operates under its own privacy policy — exactly as if you
had started the call there yourself.
