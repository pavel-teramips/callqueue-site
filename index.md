# CallQueue 📞

**People are waiting for your call. CallQueue makes sure you actually call them —
the moment you can.**

You know the drill: someone messages *"call me when you're free"*, you think
*"sure, when I'm driving"* — and then you drive, and you forget, and three days
later it's awkward. CallQueue is a tiny Android app that fixes exactly this.

## How it works

1. **Someone asks for a call → 3 seconds to queue them.** Pick them from your
   contacts, or share their chat/contact card straight into the app. Optionally
   add a deadline ("before 18:00") and a note ("she asked about the invoice").
2. **You get in the car → the app taps you on the shoulder.** The moment your
   phone connects to your car's Bluetooth, a notification appears: *"3 people
   are waiting for your call."*
3. **One tap → the call starts.** Tap a person and a **WhatsApp voice call
   fires instantly**. Not "opens WhatsApp". Not "opens the chat". The call is
   ringing. (No WhatsApp? It falls back to a regular phone call.)
4. **Call done → queue cleans itself.** Coming back from a call, the app asks
   "Done with Maria?" — remove her with one tap, or go full autopilot:
   **"Remove, call Eugene"** rolls straight into the next call. Your drive
   becomes a playlist of calls.

## Features today

- ⚡ **Instant WhatsApp voice calls** — one tap from queue to ringing
- 🚗 **Driving trigger** — car Bluetooth connects → "who's waiting" notification
- ⏰ **Deadlines** — "before 18:00"; expired entries grey out and sink down
- 📝 **Notes** — remember *why* you're calling
- 📌 **Pin** — keep the important person on top
- 📥 **Share to queue** — share a contact from any app, they're queued
- 👆 **Swipe to remove**, with Undo
- 🔒 **100% private** — everything stays on your phone; no account, no cloud,
  no tracking, nothing leaves the device
