# CallQueue 📞

**A queue of people waiting for your call. One tap calls them back.**

- "Call me later" → add them in 3 seconds, from contacts or straight from WhatsApp
- Get in the car → notification: *"3 people are waiting for your call"*
- Tap a name → **WhatsApp voice call starts instantly**
- Hang up → "Done with Maria?" → removed — or roll straight into the next call

## Features

- ⚡ One-tap WhatsApp voice calls (falls back to regular calls)
- 🚗 Car Bluetooth trigger — know who's waiting the moment you start driving
- ⏰ Deadlines — "before 18:00"; expired entries grey out
- 📝 Notes — remember why you're calling
- 📌 Pin important people · 👆 swipe to remove, with undo
- 📥 Share any contact into the queue
- 🔒 Everything stays on your phone — no account, no cloud, no tracking
